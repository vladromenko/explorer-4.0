| **ACT (Zhao et al.)** predicts joint-action chunks from images and proprioception. Its CVAE and temporal ensemble address variation in demonstrations and repeated observations. **Does one more image help when the target moves?** Diffusion Policy updates its action sequence from new observations during motion. Explorer tested a narrower adaptation on the eight previously opened development scenes 940100–940107. After the calibrated camera look, the cube moved exactly 10 mm in alternating Y directions or stayed still. Each pair started from the same complete MuJoCo and controller state, with the same simulated clock, frozen spline and ROSMASTER native limits. Only the XY supplied to the motor plan differed. There were 32 rollouts. [Upstream feedback method](https://diffusion-policy.cs.columbia.edu/) · [Frozen protocol](results/conditioned_grasp_v1/look-refresh/protocol.json) · [Branching and scoring code](../scripts/grasp_study/refresh.py#L121)

| Development condition | Terminal holds using the first image | Terminal holds after one new image | Gained / lost | Paired exact p |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Static target, all 8 scenes | 7/8 | 7/8 | 0 / 0 | 1.00 |
| Shifted target, all 8 scenes | 4/8 | 7/8 | 3 / 0 | 0.25 |
| Shifted target, both centres inside the demonstration hull | 3/5 | 5/5 | 2 / 0 | 0.50 |

Mean XY error after displacement fell from 10.49 to 5.48 mm. Three original scene centres already lay outside the demonstration hull. The native guard stopped zero rollouts. On Homebrain with CPU affinity limited to two cores, RGB capture through the new plan took a median of 45.94 ms. Compute alone took a median of 41.16 ms. All 16 capture-to-plan decisions exceeded the 40 ms budget. The observed gain warrants further investigation, while both independent confirmation and the timing requirement remain open. No weights were updated and no physical trials were performed. The first implementation stopped on a numeric precision mismatch against the historical trajectory bank. After restoring its original float64 scaling, all eight first-image plans matched the archive byte for byte and all 32 rollouts were run again. ## Public weights and camera compatibility

The Explorer SO-101 recording profile expects wrist RGB at 30 Hz, five joint angles in degrees and a calibrated gripper percentage. ROSMASTER records arm-mounted RGB at 20 Hz and six native servo coordinates. Exact units, camera pose, initial state, joint order, gripper polarity and source normalization all affect compatibility. The current draft builder accepts a completed same-profile local job as the source for fine-tuning. A separate external-initialization path should register a revision, model and processor hashes, source dataset, output semantics and licensed use, without importing external recordings into Explorer's sealed splits. ## Simulation, reinforcement learning and dataset reuse

| Source and local implementation | What to change and how to judge it |
| --- | --- |
| MuJoCo Playground and RSL-RL supply maintained training environments and runners, including work on legged platforms. Explorer pins both. Its native physics teacher uses upstream SB3 PPO and VecNormalize. SimLab also implements its own asymmetric PPO, normalization and history stacking. The two paths have different observations, privileges, engines and objectives. [Playground](https://github.com/google-deepmind/mujoco_playground) · [RSL-RL](https://github.com/leggedrobotics/rsl_rl) · [SB3](https://github.com/DLR-RM/stable-baselines3) · [Native teacher](../explorer4/sim/ppo.py) · [SimLab PPO](../explorer4/simlab/ppo.py) · [Pinned versions](../configs/sim-requirements.lock.txt) | Keep the native teacher as a privileged upper reference. Where the vectorized environment can expose separate actor and critic observations, compare the existing asymmetric trainer to an official RSL-RL runner before maintaining a second implementation. Freeze reward, observations, control rate, resets and environment steps. Compare wall time and held-out CPU MuJoCo success. A quadruped checkpoint outputs leg actions that neither tracked nor mecanum bases support. Reuse the training software and methodology. |
| Appearance randomization addresses visual shift. Dynamics randomization addresses uncertainty in actuation, contact or motion. Explorer can already randomize physics and scene appearance by seed, fit actuator lag and audit penetration. A contact model adjusts solver behavior. A calibrated camera or measured tracked-base slip requires physical data. [Tobin et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.06907) · [Peng et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.06537) · [Seeded variation](../explorer4/sim/variation.py) · [System identification](../explorer4/sim/rosmaster_sysid.py) · [Contact audit](../explorer4/sim/contact_audit.py) | Keep a nominal baseline, vary one source of error at a time on development scenes and estimate physical ranges from independent measurements. Promote a parameter fit only when it improves held-out physical trajectories or image reprojections. Repeat the frozen sim comparison under these ranges, with the same observation contract as the robot. |
| Maddukuri et al. investigate co-training on real and simulated demonstrations. Explorer already caps supplementary simulated frames against the real training frames. The official HIL-SERL implementation combines demonstrations, intervention and a reward signal. Both methods need task-specific observations and supervision. [Sim-and-real co-training](https://co-training.github.io/) · [HIL-SERL](https://github.com/rail-berkeley/hil-serl) · [LeRobot workflow](https://huggingface.co/docs/lerobot/hilserl) · [Dataset mix](../explorer4/datasets.py#L119) | Compare existing real training takes alone versus the same takes plus simulation. Disclose both training frame budgets, keep the same development trials and reserve separate physical verification. Use recorded recovery labels only when traceable to the expert. Real online RL would also need a validated reward and an available intervenor. Offline mixed-data evaluation has priority for this article. |
