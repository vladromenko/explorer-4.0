## 1. ROSMASTER takes 50 demonstrations from the floor (E1.5 h)

1. "Robots" ♪ Explorer 4 · Collection of the dataset.
2. Fill in the series form:
   - **Task:** `ROSMASTER · взять с пола (блок 3)`;
   - **Flow variation:** `rosmaster_pick_place`;
   - **Description:** Start with `взять с пола:`, e.g. `взять с пола: кубик, 7 см впереди, по центру`;
   - **The team:** Check that `pick up the cube` is set up.
3. Press **New Series**. The ROSMASTER panel will open below.
4. Connect the lead arm SO-101 to Homebrain. In the ROSMASTER panel, press **"Super mirror"** and hold the lead arm still: the ROSMASTER arm will rise in the same position, continue to repeat your movements.
5. ROSMASTER is standing on the ground. The object is lying **5–10 cm in front**, a shift to the left or right to 5 cm.
6. One display:
   1. ROSMASTER's hand is in the view position, the capture is open.
   2. **F9**.
   3. With your lead hand, lower your hand to ROSMASTER above the object, then down below, squeeze the seizure for the object.
   4. Raise your hand in the carry-over position: as a view, but with a closed hold.
   5. The object is holding up - **F10**.
7. Five sets of display, 10 sets of setup: cube, cookie, bottle, sock in 2–3 places.

The lead hand is one: while it controls ROSMASTER, SO-101 is not controlled.
