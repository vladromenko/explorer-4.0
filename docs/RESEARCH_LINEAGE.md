# Which published methods changed Explorer

Explorer tests whether a mobile robot can recognize that its own observation is insufficient, request a measurement from another robot and use the reply to complete a task. A stable simulated carry, a settled placement and a dock are three different outcomes. All closed-loop counts below are from MuJoCo. Human recordings and live sensor checks address transfer separately.

The review follows the three hardware interfaces and the article’s communication question. Sources were selected for an inspectable implementation or checkpoint, a matching sensor or action interface, or an experimental control that could distinguish two explanations in Explorer. Imported code, comparison methods and candidates awaiting data are identified in the individual rows.

At Explorer's 20 Hz this is five seconds between fresh plans. We kept the learned weights fixed, varied the execution prefix on development scenes and applied the selected length to a separate bank. [Raw policy worker](../explorer4/sim/policy_worker.py) · [Experiment and selection rule](../explorer4/recorded_start_improvement.py) | On 12 development starts, prefixes of 100, 25 and 10 yielded 4, 5 and 1 stable holds. On a separate 20 starts, prefixes of 100 and 25 yielded 5 and 6. Shortening the prefix alone gave a small measured difference. Fifty demonstrations in the source paper do not set a required count for this arm. The localizer had 1,024 labelled simulator images in addition to 32 movement demonstrations. These results justify retaining the simple spline control. ## Other reusable systems considered

Mobile ALOHA's stationary/mobile co-training is relevant when action representations and task semantics align. MimicGen provides demonstrated subtask composition with object frames and termination cues. A local cube-on-floor task with five verified physical training takes lacks the verified pose annotations needed for that substitution. NoMaD/ViNT predict visual waypoints on mobile platforms; they would require target-image episodes, verified wheel-control conventions and an appropriate comparison to geometric navigation. Each entry in the detailed register records the specific prerequisites and code that would change.
