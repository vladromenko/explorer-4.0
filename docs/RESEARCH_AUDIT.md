# Selective help and motor transfer on Homebrain

## Research question

When a tracked robot cannot locate its dock, does choosing between further local observation and a specific available camera improve the recipient's terminal outcome per unit of elapsed time and helper service, compared with searching locally first? The unit of replication is a scene. An independent verifier scores the final position, heading and stop. The fixed comparator gets the same sensing deadline and the same service offers as the adaptive policy. [Paired experiment](../scripts/peer_action_value_pilot.py) · [Event and cost reconstruction](../scripts/peer_action_value_evidence.py)

On the 16 confirmation scenes the trained and fixed local-first selectors both docked 13 times with six calls. Local-only docked eight times. Asking at the first opportunity docked twelve times with twelve calls and spoiled one otherwise successful attempt. The original fit and calibration records were recovered from their hashed files after an RNG-state mismatch. Restoring the saved process RNG states reproduced the checked reset snapshots byte for byte. [Complete event evidence](results/research-audit/trials/common-budget-events.json) · [Reset reproduction](http://192.168.0.168:8030/simlab-media/reports/peer-action-value-v1/reset-reproduction.json)

The second question is whether a verified SO-101 grasp lets ROSMASTER acquire the movement with fewer independent target demonstrations or fewer total human minutes. A donor observation or a narrower J6 error cannot establish this claim. The endpoint requires an independently scored recipient grasp with the donor disconnected. The five complete archival takes and six usable prefixes are a technical pilot. A separate field evaluation and a verified donor packet are still needed. [Donor audit](results/so101_donor_audit_20261116.json) · [Measured donor packet](../explorer4/skill_packets.py) · [Transfer using recipient IK](../explorer4/sim/skill_transfer.py)

## What each hypothesis can establish

| Hypothesis | Data, strong comparator and falsification criterion |
| --- | --- |
| An additional robot's view sometimes changes the terminal result | 24 paired MuJoCo docking starts with the same recipient, fresh, expired and swapped messages. Against active local sensing, the original 24 open-view scenes show no additional successes. Occluding the view makes the report useful in the screened development stratum. A request count alone cannot establish benefit. Physical camera, transform and end-position measurements remain outstanding. [Docking evidence](results/peer-camera-docking/paired.csv) |
| An action-value decision improves on local-first under variable costs and faults | Train on 24 scenes, calibrate on 16 other scenes, freeze the fit, compare on a third room with 16 new scenes. Both arms have six seconds for sensing and twenty overall. The primary outcome subtracts the registered costs from terminal success. Lower paired utility or a trade-off sensitive to plausible prices limits the claim. Availability, delay and the physical cost of a particular helper have to be observed for deployment. [Registered pilot](http://192.168.0.168:8030/simlab-media/reports/peer-action-value-v1/protocol.json) |
| More precise wrist localization improves native grasping | The RGB keypoint network and original estimator used the same 1,024 simulated training images, frozen look pose and 32 motor demonstrations. The network reduced median and tail error on 48 new scenes. Unrestricted terminal holds fell from 29 to 27. On the previously opened severe miss the new output correctly fell outside motor support. Replacing the estimator requires an independent task-level gain. [Fit, paired rows and image](results/conditioned_grasp_v1/wrist-heatmap/result.json) |
| Adjusting motor targets improves real-executor retention | Fit nine small corrections on 16 eligible training scenes. Confirm on the first 32 eligible fresh starts under the same native 360-tick actuator path. Baseline bilateral terminal carry was 31/32, fitted 29/32. The original strict jaw endpoint also fell, 23/32 to 20/32. These conditional simulated rates do not predict a physical success rate. [All paired outcomes](results/conditioned_grasp_v1/native-residual/result.json) |
| A detector can choose a useful recovery | Compare a template tracker and a scalar probe of actual RGB features. Fit and calibrate on separate episodes. Report false alarms, missed failures, lead to failure and the terminal effects of executing recovery. On 32 fresh starts routine reobservation raised first stable carries from 21 to 28 (eight gains, one loss). Both alarms missed all eleven original failures. The probe raised five false alarms. All 32 pairs used the separately registered common-exposure recovery after the first raw rerender failed the strict image check. [Registered branches](../scripts/grasp_study/failure_watch.py) · [Earlier moving-camera development](results/conditioned_grasp_v1/moving-recovery.json) |
| Donor movement reduces target training cost | Match target takes, updates, sensors, initialization and synthetic budgets. Compare a relevant donor to generic simulation, shuffled donor, geometry alone and target-only. Receiver-only confirmation and total person-time are required. Without a calibrated successful physical donor, measured collection times and recipient trials this remains an open question. [Transfer preflight](results/skill_transfer_preflight_20261116/summary.json) |

The primary peer confirmation consumed 320.85 process CPU seconds across 64 measured branches. Their combined simulated task time was 569.85 seconds. Each selective policy accumulated 18 simulated seconds of camera service and four seconds of waiting. The donor base stayed fixed. Physical travel, human setup time and Jetson energy use were unmeasured. The paper uses the independently terminated scene outcomes and reports sensitivity to the registered helper and time prices. ## Prior work and implementation decisions

| Primary work | Reusable idea, public code or weights, and consequence on this host |
| --- | --- |
| UPS, RSS 2026 | Separates semantic ambiguity, feasible actions and intervention. Its released pipeline uses a Dreamer-style model and a Gemini verifier. Explorer uses local measured-outcome forecasts and a bounded fallback. We adopted the question of which intervention changes the outcome. The upstream verifier and world model were not executed. [Paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22474) · [Inspected upstream revision](https://github.com/CMU-IntentLab/uncertainty_aware_policy_steering/tree/f7bad4d7cd6d5e4350751a958ba9bb146a99ecec) |
| Ask-type multi-agent POMDP, RA-L 2026 | Communication has a value conditional on information available to the agents. [Author manuscript](https://cse-robotics.engr.tamu.edu/dshell/papers/ral2026comms.pdf) · [Published article](https://doi.org/10.1109/LRA.2026.3703246) |
| FIPER and SAFE, NeurIPS 2025 | FIPER uses generative action-chunk entropy and observation novelty, calibrated on successful rollouts. The executed RBF planner is deterministic. Repeating its command would yield no meaningful entropy. SAFE fits a scalar detector on VLA internals using labelled rollouts. We adapt its probe idea to two measured RGB feature observations and independent episode calibration, with a simple tracker as control. This is a different architecture and a single skill. [FIPER](https://tum-lsy.github.io/fiper_website/) · [FIPER code](https://github.com/learnsyslab/fiper) · [SAFE](https://vla-safe.github.io/) · [SAFE code](https://github.com/vla-safe/SAFE) |

| SmolVLA and X-VLA | SmolVLA has 450,046,176 published parameters and a six-channel output. It already ran here with target-domain processing. Execution needs a measured action conversion, camera contract and latency benchmark. Published weight size alone cannot establish training or inference memory. Both model cards declare Apache-2.0.
